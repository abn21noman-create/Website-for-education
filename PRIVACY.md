# Nokkhotro HSC ’28 Privacy Policy

**Effective and last updated: 7 November 2026**

Public policy URL: <https://nokkhotro-hsc-2028.vercel.app/privacy>

Public account-deletion URL: <https://nokkhotro-hsc-2028.vercel.app/account-deletion>

This policy applies to the Nokkhotro HSC ’28 website, installable web app, and Android app (together, **Nokkhotro**). Privacy requests can be sent to **abn21.noman@gmail.com**. Do not send a password, access token, or complete study-data export by email.

## Information processed

- **Account and profile:** email address, authentication identifiers, name, optional college, education board, study target, account dates, and sign-in status.
- **Private learning and life data:** syllabus progress, quiz results, bookmarks, notes, tasks, study sessions, routines, goals, mistakes, focus minutes, habits, attendance, deadlines, exam plans/results, and accessibility preferences.
- **Community data:** questions, replies, votes, and the name/college/board shown with a post. Community XP/profile summaries appear only after explicit opt-in.
- **Student pulse:** five constrained multiple-choice answers linked to the account for update/deletion. Administrators receive only threshold-protected aggregate totals, never individual response rows.
- **Service and security data:** hosting and authentication providers may process IP address, browser/app version, request time, and diagnostic or abuse-prevention logs needed to operate and protect the service.

Nokkhotro does **not** read the list of installed apps, which other app a student opened, screen content, contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone. Android Focus Lock state and the live countdown stay on the device. Nokkhotro has no ads or third-party advertising trackers.

## Use, storage, and sharing

Information is used to create and secure accounts; sync and restore study data; calculate progress; provide requested community features; answer support or security reports; prevent abuse; and maintain the service. Personal information is not sold, and private study content is not used for advertising.

The browser or Android WebView keeps account-scoped copies and an offline write queue. Signed-in data is synced over encrypted HTTPS to Supabase. Vercel delivers the website. These providers may process data in countries where they operate, including the configured Supabase Asia-Pacific region.

Private study records are owner-only. Authorized administrators receive a limited account directory (such as email, profile name, status, last sign-in, XP, and study-minute summary), but not passwords, access tokens, personal notes, routines, or private study documents. Community posts and replies are visible to signed-in students. XP profile sharing requires explicit opt-in.

## Retention and deletion

Live account and study data is kept while an account remains active. A completed full-account deletion removes the Supabase Auth user, profile, private study/life data, student-pulse row, leaderboard participation, administrator membership/allowlist entry if applicable, authored questions/replies, and votes from the live service. A thread started by a deleted user is removed, so replies inside that thread may also disappear.

Limited residual copies may remain temporarily in encrypted provider backups or security logs for the provider’s rolling operational retention period, or longer where required to investigate abuse or comply with law. They are not available through the app and are overwritten or deleted under provider schedules. User-exported files, Dropbox copies, and copies on another offline device are controlled by the user and cannot be remotely erased by Nokkhotro.

A student can:

1. edit profile details and optional leaderboard consent in the app;
2. use **Reset study data** to erase private study records while keeping the account and community contributions;
3. use **Delete account and all data** in the app; or
4. sign in and initiate deletion on the public [account-deletion page](https://nokkhotro-hsc-2028.vercel.app/account-deletion).

## Security

Nokkhotro uses encrypted transport, Supabase Row Level Security, account-scoped local spaces, server-authoritative administrator roles, restrictive web security headers, and a server-side deletion function. No service-role secret is included in the website or Android bundle. No system is perfectly secure; suspected security issues should be reported privately under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Teen and young users

Nokkhotro is designed for HSC learners, including teenagers. It is not intended for children under 13. A parent or guardian may contact the maintainer if they believe a younger child supplied personal information; after reasonable verification, it will be deleted. Teen users should involve a guardian where local law requires consent.

## Changes and contact

Material changes will be dated on the public policy page. Questions, access/deletion problems, or guardian requests can be sent to **abn21.noman@gmail.com**.
